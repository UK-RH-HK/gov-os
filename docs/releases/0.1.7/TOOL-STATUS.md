- **green:** proven in real use, no open finding that fails open or loses work in ordinary use. Use it now; judge a run by the record in "It must produce", never by the exit code (DEC-1240).
- **amber:** built, not proven. Do the step by hand (the plain path), as the last column says.
- **not yet:** not in the product. Use the equivalent in the last column.

**How a tool moves (TL-11).** Up, amber or not yet to green, only once the integration has proven it on the scratch repository; products gain it by `gov update`. Down, green to amber, at once, in a patch release, when a finding marked "blocks or loses work: yes" names a green tool (the findings log's column; Gov OS's `FINDINGS.md` does not have it yet, so until then the CTO judges each finding).

Nothing was run to prove a tool for this list: each status rests on the record its row names. It is the list of `proposals` §1 re-read against 0.1.7 and the findings up to I-42. Fixes made after the cut are not in 0.1.7.

**Counts: 17 green, 18 amber, 17 not yet, of 52.** One change of status from the proposals' list (17, 17, 18): TL12, not yet to amber (C3).

**Short names.** "TOOL-MAP row n", "RECORDS-MAP" and "plan n-n" are `TOOL-MAP.md`, `RECORDS-MAP.md` and `INTEGRATION-PLAN.md`, and "I-n" is `FINDINGS.md`, all in `docs/emergency-2/integration/`; "A-n" is List A of `docs/emergency-2/audits/E2-AUDIT-4-DETERMINISM.md`; "TL-n" is a card of DEC-1252 and "C3", "C4" a ruling in `PRACTICE-FIRST-PLAN.md` §3; "proposals" is `docs/emergency-2/proposals/PRACTICE-FIRST-PROPOSALS.md`; a name like `phase0-memory-20261124` is a landing record in Gov OS's untracked `.gov-runtime/scratch/orchestrator/log/landings/`.
