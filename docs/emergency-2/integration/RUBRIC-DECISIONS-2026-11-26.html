Seventeen choices for the classification rubric you ordered before W2-213's spec (DEC-1255 (3)), in five sections: the classes and what memory holds, the standard layout, how the classing runs, what needs your yes, and the conflicts with what stands. The rubric itself is docs/emergency-2/proposals/CLASSIFICATION-RUBRIC.md. Each card gives what was found, the options with their costs, and one recommendation with its reason. Pick an option, add a note if you want, and copy the answer sheet at the end. Your answers are kept on this device as you go.

How to answer

  • Tap one option on a card. A card you leave alone is sent as "skip".
  • "Take every recommendation" fills every card with its recommended option; your notes stay.
  • At the end, tap "Copy the answer sheet" and paste it to your advisor, then to the CTO.

Your answer sheet

Taking the recommendations keeps any notes you've written. A note is sent as one line, 400 characters at most.
