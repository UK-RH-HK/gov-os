Three decisions from the night of 25 to 26 November, in two sections. E-01 and E-02 hold batch A of the 0.1.8 bundle; E-03 is what 0.1.8 holds. Each card gives what was found, the options with their costs, and one recommendation with its reason. Pick an option, add a note if you want, and copy the answer sheet at the end. Your answers are kept on this device as you go.

How to answer

  • Tap one option on a card. A card you leave alone is sent as "skip".
  • "Take every recommendation" fills every card with its recommended option; your notes stay.
  • At the end, tap "Copy the answer sheet" and paste it to your advisor, then to the CTO.

Your answer sheet

Taking the recommendations keeps any notes you've written. A note is sent as one line, 400 characters at most.
