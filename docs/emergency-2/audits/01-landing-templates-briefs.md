## 5. Findings the tables cannot say

- **The landing is slow because of its selection, not its checks.** `select_tests.py` uses `plan`, and `plan` always runs all of `tests/unit` (6,853 tests). That is why `lead_verify` (22.5 min) and the land (21 min) take about the same time and catch the same reds. P2 describes `lead_verify` as running the full suite on the branch; in fact it runs this same wide selection. The product's import-graph `scoped` selection already exists and is unused by the Gov OS landing.
- **Retiring the guard removes the product's path check.** `gov close` judges containment through the guard's `decide` (command.py:613; containment.py:1692-1705). Retire `src/gov/guard` without a replacement and the close loses its path check. That fails open on allowed-path drift, which P2 counts as an ordinary-work catch (11 ejections).
- **Two places still require a review for FULL tickets**: `land.py` precheck and `gov close`'s `review_verdict`. B-2 / 3.6 says no ticket gets a reviewer. Until both change, a FULL ticket cannot land without one.
- **Most of the landing is scratch code.** `land.py`, `select_tests.py`, `landing_select.sh`, `lead_verify.sh`, `kernel_gate.py`, `verdict.py` and `close_build.py` sit under the git-ignored `.gov-runtime/` and are not versioned (P4 R14 assumed they were). Audit 5 should know this.
- **An installed release carries 2 of the 22 templates and 2 of the 19 skills.** The rest reach an adopted repository only through the Copier kit (`pyproject.toml:25-28`). `gov update` (W2-17) must carry the whole kernel, or `gov brief` refuses with BRIEF_TEMPLATE_MISSING.

## Decisions for the owner

1. **W2-115b `gov queue` for the release: how much?**
   - (a) Port `merge_queue.py` whole into `src/gov/queue`: warm clones, store seeding and promotion, records-only head moves. About 1,400 lines; the most work and the most to test.
   - (b) A thin `gov queue` on `batch.isolate`, `select.scoped` and the close: batch, culprit out, fast-forward to the tested merge, close at the land. About 300 to 500 lines; Gov OS's speed-ups stay out until friction asks for them.
   - (c) No queue for the release: `gov close` lands one ticket at a time (merge, scoped run, path and author checks, close). About 200 to 300 lines; no batching, and EXAMIN's Wave 0 runs one stream anyway.
   - Recommendation: **(b)**. It meets A-4's reason ("the landing must exist in adopted repositories") at about a third of (a)'s size, and Gov OS can move onto it as a real proving run.
2. **DONE by code (DEC-1052): what counts as DONE now that "the ticket closes at the land" (4.3 step 5)?**
   - (a) Build a `gov done` that reads DOD-1 to DOD-6 and answers DONE. About 150 lines, and it keeps two lines that conflict with 3.6 and 2.2.
   - (b) DONE is the green landing itself: accept green, the land's checks green, the close written. The DOD lines shrink to those three, and DOD-4 and DOD-6 go. About 30 lines of template and facts edits.
   - (c) Leave DONE as text. No cost, and DEC-1052 stays unmet.
   - Recommendation: **(b)**.
