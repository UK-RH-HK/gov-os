## Question

Six consecutive rejections (4.1.3, 4.1.4, 4.1.5, RoT-1 revisions 1, 2 and 3) share one class: a lower-trust input
yielding a current, higher-trust fact. Revision 3's instances were:
- a kernel's precedence rules, or their absence, deciding the project's effective controls;
- an anchor satisfied by a sequence number the supplier chooses, and an anchor treated as current indefinitely;
- a threshold-1 final release naming the source of the trusted computing base.

Which root of trust governs every Governance OS kernel, release and binary ingress and use?

## Why earlier corrections left a remainder

`28` gives the full account. Each revision added conditions downstream of a choice that a lower-trust party still made.
Revision 4 removes those inputs from the decision:
- the kernel from effective precedence;
- absence from the semantics of registered content;
- the supplier's sequence number from anchor satisfaction;
- aged anchors from trust ingress;
- the trust-state key from currency;
- `release-final` from the choice of a binary's source;
- the governed account from the writers of honoured pins.

## Consequences (abridged)

- **Constitutional content.**
  - Every file and leaf is classified by the root-signed Constitutional Surface.
  - Unknown content is denied, and registered content must be present.
  - Kernel precedence must equal its registration, and the project layer uses only registered precedence.
  - Project strength is evaluated over effective policy, whatever changed it.
- **Freshness and currency.**
  - Anchors are satisfied only by inclusion, and pins expire.
  - Trust ingress and binary acceptance need a currency proof; an in-gate typed state fingerprint needs no clock.
  - No surface says `current`.
- **Binaries.** A binary is accepted only with all of these: two `release-artifact` signatures, an independent build
  attestation, a source attested by an independent verification and equal in candidate, final, build attestation and
  TBM, a TSS reference, and a Trust Base Manifest at or above the accepted-TBM high-water.
- **Trust decisions.** Local VTS confirmations, or protected, expiring decision pins. Repository gate records are
  requests, and `gov`-run repository commands cannot write pins.
- **Legacy binaries.**
  - While the layout is intact, pre-RoT binaries write nothing (executed against 4.1.2–4.1.5, re-run unchanged).
  - After occupation removal or a Git restore, RoT-1 fails closed and reports lost strength where recorded (LR-2).
- **Consumers.** Legacy consumers update through one anchored, locally gated transaction that migrates the layout.
