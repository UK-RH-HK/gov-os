## Question

Nine consecutive rejections share one class: a lower-trust input yielding a current, higher-trust fact. Revision 6's instances
were first-contact values composed by the trust-state publisher, designated by carriers and submitted by a package submitter;
first-contact values of unbounded age and re-admission that ignored the store; an environment manifest no party established; and
a register complete over rule ids instead of inputs. With the owner's parameters selected, which concrete root of trust governs
every Governance OS kernel, release and binary ingress and use?

## Why earlier corrections left a remainder

`28` gives the full account. Revision 6 made the register complete over the rule ids it had written; the parties that
composed, designated, submitted or authored a value were invisible to it, and the option tree multiplied the combinations in
which they mattered. Revision 7 checks completeness over values and establishing parties, and has one profile.

## Consequences (abridged)

- **First contact.** A First-Contact Authority record at root threshold; two sources under separate custody that publish codes
  only after first-hand verification; designation from the ceremony record at onboarding; a compiled admitter under a 24-hour
  state age; re-admission never below held state.
- **Releases and binaries.** 2-of-3 delegated registration; two verification records; environment lock in source and derived
  manifests; 2-of-3 reproduction across independent supplier classes and toolchain lineages; registered binary digests; a
  computed security minimum.
- **Running machines.** Anchors valid 90 days (CI 7 days); C3 needs currency of at most 24 hours from both sources; a clock below
  the recorded high-water leaves read-only diagnostics; every admission record expires; a revoked binary runs read-only
  diagnostics only; local trust gates for every installation, update, rollback and recovery.
- **Legacy binaries.** Unchanged containment; property R2-H4 re-run at 0 violations; the first-install layout migration is
  journaled step by step.
- **Targets.** Only certified targets have production admission; the initial set is not certified pending criteria (OT-2).
