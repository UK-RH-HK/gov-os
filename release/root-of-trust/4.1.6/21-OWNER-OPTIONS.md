## OP-3 — Gating for adoption, update and rollback

- **Mode A** (`always_gate`): every trust transition needs the local trust gate.
- **Mode B**: certified updates skip the human gate. **They do not skip currency** (RV5-L9). Under CR4-B-07 option 1 a P1
  proof names only the anchored Trust State. So a mode-B machine anchored at t11 refuses an update published in t12
  (`TRUST_STATE_CURRENCY_UNPROVEN`) until one of three things arrives: a fresh confirmation or pin naming t12, a typed
  fingerprint of t12, or OP-7 (c) witnesses on t12. Evidence: P4r6 `R6-OP3-B-*`. The human step is removed entirely only
  under OP-7 (c).
- Unchanged: mode A's "never answerable by an agent path" holds for `gov decide` and every confined child; an agent with an
  unconfined shell in the same account is A3.

## OP-7 — Currency for machines without a current proof

Choices and parameters as `24` §9. Consequences:
- **Stateful machines.** A clock set back below the high-water fails closed. A statement refused as issued in the future
  makes clock-based proofs unusable for that unit of work, so a restored store whose clock is set back fails closed when a
  newer statement is delivered (CR5-B-08; P4r6 `R6-CLOCK-*`). The residual is RS-2b: a restored store with the clock set back
  **and** every newer statement withheld cannot distinguish the past (`24` §10).
- **Machines without a verifier trust store.** Pin validity and the C3 window rest on TA-7 (RS-2).
- **(c).** Witness input from the ceremony or the source only; the C3 threshold under two custodians, or `draft-policy`
  flags the one-custody consequence (victim WR rows of OP-9).
- **First admission** does not depend on OP-7.
- **User-writable installations** (RV5-M8, revision 6 option (a); `31` §7.1, UW6):

  | OP-7 answer | Classes reachable on a user-writable installation | Burden for governed use |
  |---|---|---|
  | (a), (b), (c) without witnesses held | C0 | an administrator-protected installation or an administrator-provisioned system pin on every workstation that governs work |
  | (c) with witnesses at the C3 threshold | C0–C2 | witness service availability; C3 still needs a protected installation |
  | (d) | C0–C2, labelled `FRESHNESS_UNPROVEN` | the (d) residual; C3 still needs a protected installation |

  A legacy consumer on a user-writable workstation cannot complete Phase 4 `update --apply` (C3) until a protected
  installation exists (`11` Phase 4).
