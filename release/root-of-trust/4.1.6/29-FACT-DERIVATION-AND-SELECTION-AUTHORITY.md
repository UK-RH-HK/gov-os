through shapes no rule forbade:

## 2. The rule

**FD-1 (fact derivation and selection authority).** For every trust decision *D* that confers authority α(*D*) and currency
κ(*D*):

1. **Roles.** Every input of *D* — every field of every statement, manifest and local record, and every procedure input — has
   exactly one role in the decision register (§4):
   - a **selector**, whose value determines which of several authentic candidates becomes the effective fact;
   - a **restrictor**, which can only refuse, or strengthen through a monotone join;
   - a **binding**, a value derived deterministically from other registered inputs, refused when it differs from the derivation;
   - a **carrier**, which transports bytes whose identity a selector already fixed;
   - **informational**, shown and read by no decision;
   - **excluded**, a mode of an unselected option, absent or refused in CP-1 (`35` §4).
2. **Selector authority.** Every selector MUST have authority ≥ α(*D*) and currency ≥ κ(*D*) (§3), and the register names the
   party that **establishes** its value. Restrictors may come from any authenticated input. Carriers may come from anywhere.
3. **First-hand establishment.** A signature contributes to a selector's authority only for a fact its signer established
   itself.
4. **No self-evaluation.** The artefact under judgement is never a selector, and never the evaluator of its own acceptance.
5. **Shortfall.** A decision whose selector cannot be supplied at the required authority and currency fails closed, unless the
   pack states the shortfall as a residual with an exact bound, a label on every surface that shows the state, and an acceptance
   test that fails when the bound is exceeded. The register marks such a decision `shortfall` (DR-15, DR-33). The first-contact
   root is the remainder of DR-02…DR-06 and DR-36…DR-39, stated in `32` §9.

**FD-2 (derived strength).** The strength of a fact is the minimum, over every input that can change or select it, of that
input's authority. Where a derivation can be enumerated, the minimum is **computed**, never argued (FD-3).

**FD-3 (computed minima).** Every statement of the pack of the form "an accepted malicious binary (or effective content, or an
admitted revoked binary) needs at least …" MUST be the output of the derivation calculator (§5.3) for CP-1, with pipeline,
publication and infrastructure inputs as atoms. Hand-enumerated routes are not evidence (§5.5).

## 3. Authority and currency

**Authority** (highest first):

| Rank | Authority |
|---|---|
| 1 | the root threshold (2 of 3) |
| 2 | a quorum of at least two distinct single-purpose keys each establishing the fact first-hand; or local machine authority: a human typing values read now from both first-contact sources, a protected operator pin within its validity, a local confirmation |
| 3 | one key of one purpose |
| 4 | the repository writer, project configuration, the governed account |
| 5 | transport, unauthenticated input, the pipeline, the artefact under judgement |

**Currency** (highest first): established now (typed in the gate or at first contact) > within 24 hours (a pin or confirmation
naming that state; a first-contact state) > as of an anchor within its validity (90 days workstation, 7 days CI) > none.

Custody independence (different people, organisations, build environments, first-contact sources, supplier provenance) is
recorded in the root ceremony record and the root-signed registries; where a rank relies on it, it is a stated assumption
(TA-4, TA-5′, TA-10′, TA-11, TA-12″).

leaf field of every certified schema (`inputs`), every procedure input (`procedure_inputs`) and every exclusion

- **C9 input completeness.** Every leaf field of every certified schema has a role; selectors, restrictors and bindings name the
  establishing party and a decision; a whole schema may be covered by one row only as carrier, informational or excluded, with

- **C10 procedure inputs.** Every procedure input has a role, a decision and a test whose input is an executed scenario, and the
  kinds review r6 found unregistered are each present.

### 5.2 On every root version: the Fact Threshold Check and CP-1 shapes (`05` §3)

SV-4 refuses a root version whose grants deviate from the CP-1 shapes (KS-1″, KS-2, KS-9′, KS-10″, KS-12…KS-18) or let a key
hold a second purpose outside the three root-held pairs. A later version gives `ROOT_VERSION_INVALID`; a root v1 gives
`ROOT_CHAIN_INVALID` or `PROFILE_NONCONFORMANT`. No owner field changes these shapes.

### 5.3 Before a root ceremony: the derivation calculator (FD-3)

`gov trust draft-policy --derivation` enumerates the owner's release process with honest parties acting only on first-hand
checks. It computes the minimal capability sets for each attack goal and victim class of CP-1 and fails the draft when an
invariant fails. The reference is `evidence/r7/CS7-derivation-calculator.py`:
- **Method.** Exact minimal true sets by hypergraph dualisation with no size bound. Revision 7 maintains the transversals
  incrementally; the result equals the revision-6 recomputing enumeration wherever that finishes (`enumeration_reference_checks`).
  Monotonicity is spot-checked.
- **Atoms.** Processes, keys, infrastructure (pipeline, transport, repository writer, mirror, trust-state publication process,
  carrier), first-contact capabilities (`src1`, `src2`, `desig1`, `desig2`), stored codes, and residual assumptions. Every atom
  has an explicit class; the renderer is injective (RV6-M1).
- **Goals.** Malicious source, inputs, bytes, mirror, toolchain, environment, constitutional content, and a **revoked binary
  admitted** (review r6 CD6-2).
- **Victims.** P1, P1A, P2, CIR, FA, RA_held, RA_unheld; USE, ING_P1, ING_P2.
- **One production configuration.** The CP-1 parameters are fixed; no excluded mode has an atom or a branch.
- **Revision-6 control.** It switches off exactly the revision-7 rules and must reproduce review r6's findings (CP-R6-CONTROLS,
  non-production).

Results for CP-1 (configurations, self-checks, invariant checks, monotonicity checks, load-bearing rules): `22` §1.
