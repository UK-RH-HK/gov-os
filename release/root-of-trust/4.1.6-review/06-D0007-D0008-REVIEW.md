**Additional rules D-0008 must carry before approval:**

- **(11) Authenticity is not currency.** A T1 fact that is superseded, withdrawn, rejected without explicit
  acceptance, below the T0 operational floor, or not linked to fresh revocation data may not serve as a policy root or
  relax an authorisation decision. Constitutional floors never fall below the running binary's embedded authenticated
  kernel (strengthen-only).
- **(12) Only the release role confers kernel authenticity.** Certification, revocation and profile statements
  annotate or lower trust; they never create T1 content identity. Legacy identities are T0 constants.
- **(13) Negative lifecycle facts are monotonic.** Rejection, withdrawal and revocation travel in the monotonic
  revocation sequence with a compiled floor. Any positive fact used to relax a gate must be linked to a revocation
  sequence at least as new as the one it was issued under.
- **(14) Authority for an ingress never comes from the material being admitted.** It is read from T0, strengthened by
  the currently installed authenticated kernel.
