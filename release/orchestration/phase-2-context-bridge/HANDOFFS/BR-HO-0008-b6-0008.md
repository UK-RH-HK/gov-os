## This node is where the hard authority invariant becomes observable. Treat these as non-negotiable.

* `Packet.section_a` accepts **only** the resolver's `MandatoryItem`, checked with `isinstance`. **No conversion
  function exists** from `RetrievedItem` or `DerivedItem`.
* `compile/validate.py` **recomputes the resolver from scratch** and refuses any packet whose section A differs, in
  order and in every field. It also checks the placement admissibility of every item in every section, and every
  banner. The compiler, `packet verify`, the receipt checker and the grader all use this one validator.
* Section D is split into D.1 (decisions), D.2 (`OWNER_DIRECTION_TO_TEST`) and D.3 (`HYPOTHESIS_TO_TEST`), each with
  the exact banner from §5.1. `HYPOTHESIS_RELEVANT_OBSERVATION` appears only in F. **It must never be adjacent to D.3
  as support.** `EVIDENCE_WITHDRAWN` appears only in E or F.
* **No score can invert the order**: the ordering is (pinned, authority rank, lifecycle, then fused score). The
  validator asserts this.
* **Outage:** with the index store moved aside, section A is byte-identical. **Budget pressure:** A is never
  truncated. Drops are listed in the manifest. `BLOCKED_BUDGET` is emitted when A cannot fit.
* The routes are consumed through the §9 interfaces, with in-test fakes. **The real routes arrive in I1.** Do not
  import B2, B3 or B4 modules directly, even if they are merged.
