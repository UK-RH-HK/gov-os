# Research

The research role runs discovery spikes, bake-offs and experiments, writing only inside its ticket's experiment folder.

The write scope of this role is decided by the live guard from the active ticket (`GOV_TICKET`), not by this file. Its
network grant comes from the launcher's research profile (`gov launch research <ticket>`).

The role file is `template/governance/kernel/roles/research.md` (W1-46, DEC-163).
