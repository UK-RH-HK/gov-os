## Judgement on the four questions

- `tests/unit/retrieval/test_w2_92_routes.py`. The expected routes go from `["code"]` to `["lexical", "code"]`

**(2) The unguarded `chunks[hit["chunk_id"]]` cannot be reached in ordinary work (R4).** `chunks` is empty

### What was checked

- **The matcher against the live hook.** The hook was compared with the committed `.claude/hooks/role_reads.py`
  (`git show HEAD:`), not the stale copy in the working tree. `section`, `_block`, `check`, `ticket_front`,
  `linked_tests`, `_fork`, `_files_of`, `changed`, `engineer_side`, `shared`/`SHARED`, `Denied`, `Linked`,
  `expand`, `Judge.hit`, `judges`, `coordination` and `_links_unless_coordination` are the hook's logic, with
  errors wrapped as `RolePathsError`. A differential run in-process called the hook's `judges`/`Judge.hit` and the
  module's on every tracked path. It covered seven cases (role, ticket, searched tree):
  - the engineer on DAEO-zqvn in `W2-13` and in the main tree;
  - the designer on DAEO-zqvn in `W2-13`, in `W2-13-tests` and in the main tree;
  - the engineer on DAEO-0rfi (W2-54, the linked-tests case) and on DAEO-sdxk (W2-00, `class: coordination`).

  The sections were equal. Over about 22,000 judgements the two disagreed **0** times. Withheld counts were 24/24,
  22/22, 7/7, 0/0, 0/0, 32/32 and 0/0. The coordination ticket gets its own folder only (ACT-G14b). `{ticket_impl}`
  in the engineer's own tree covers only the branch's changed files, less the (c3) shared files.
- **Where it applies.** In `retrieve()` the filter runs on every route's hits (lexical, semantic, closure, code)
  before `rrf`. Everything after that is derived from `fused`: gathering, `gaps`, the rerank, the parent expansions
  (same path) and the continuation offset. So a withheld chunk never takes a rank, a gap line or a span. The
  stated-gap bundle offers nothing and carries `role_filter` too. `gov retrieve` is the only caller of the search
  routes in `src/gov`. In `gov context`, `supplementary._ranked` drops a withheld chunk before it is ranked.
  `--role` is a shared CLI option, reaches both commands (`command.py` → `context(role=)` → `stack(role=)`), and
  adds to `GOV_ROLE`; it never replaces it.
- **Named, never silent.** The bundle's `role_filter` gives the roles, the ticket, the rule, the withheld paths and
  chunks, and a breakdown by role, entry and glob, counting a chunk once across routes. The supplementary block adds
  one `dropped` entry, `role_filter`, with the counts and the rules. `gov context` sorts that entry into the
  packet's `dropped`.
- **No section, no filter.** With no section, no role the section names, or no rule the ticket fills, `caller`
  returns None and the bundle has no `role_filter`. This is unit-tested and covered by the acceptance case
  `test_no_role_paths_section_means_no_filter`.
- **Fails closed.** A section or ticket that cannot be read stops `gov retrieve` with `ROLE_PATHS_UNREADABLE`. In
