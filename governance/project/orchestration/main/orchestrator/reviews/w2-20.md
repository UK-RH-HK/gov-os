## Point 2: R2-F1's fix, verdict pass

R2-F1 is fixed. A server's kept samples are now those of its current pid that are at most 600 s old (`kept()`). A listening server with none kept is sampled twice, 0.25 s apart, before the cpu rule runs, so its `now` value is the rate between those two samples, a number. Three hours of expired history now reads 0.0 and the gate opens, which both the designer's case (`--check` and launch paths) and the unit tests confirm.

Nothing new fails open:

- **A pid change inside the 0.25 s gap:** `now` and p95 read null and the gate shuts on cpu. That fails closed, and the window is tiny.
