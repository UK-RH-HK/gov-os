## 17:45 update: the measurement, and the cause corrected
- W2-69's tests (352) through one slot on the quiet machine: without the thread budget 1,069 s, peak runnable 165, CPU
  88 % busy; with it 968 s, peak runnable 179, CPU 87 %. All passed both times. **The thread budget does not bound the
  load** (it was switched on at 17:46 anyway: reviewed PASS, 10 % faster, and it stops any multi-threaded child).
- **The cause, corrected:** `src/gov/secrets/__init__.py:599` judges files with a pool of `os.cpu_count()` (20, which
  ignores any CPU set) at once, each a gitleaks; in each of a slot's 6 xdist workers: about 120 gitleaks at once.
- **The fix proposed to the owner (17:4x):** (1) each slot's run confined to its share of the CPUs (`taskset` in
  `heavy_slot.sh`, from the brief); (2) the scan's pool sized to the CPUs it may use (`len(os.sched_getaffinity(0))`),
  an emergency patch to `src/gov/secrets`, with its unit test and one review.
