## Why they were not superseded already (evidence only)

1. **"Standing until the owner lifts it," with no lift ever recorded.** DEC-1032 ("the freeze standing until the owner lifts
   it"), DEC-1033 ("standing until the owner lifts it"), DEC-1047, DEC-1051 and DEC-1049 ("until the owner's word") each end
   only on an owner act. DEC-1039 (4) says "once green the owner lifts the emergency and the freeze". A search of the
   register finds no entry that lifts them. DEC-1126 records "the re-pin done", and ends usage (A) "at the lift", treating
   the lift as an event, but it names none of these decisions. DIF line 8 still heads its section "The emergency, until the
   owner lifts it". The only "none" is the status block's hand-kept fact (`log/report-state.json` line 34).
2. **Events that met an end condition were not written back.** DEC-1051 (2) ends "after the refactoring and the re-pins",
   and both happened (DEC-1126 (1)). The nightly came back through ACT-NIGHTLY-RESTART-1 (DEC-1132 (4), applied 11-11 18:30),
   yet DEC-1049 is unmarked and still on DIF line 12. DEC-1064's "until then" (W2-127) is met, since the ticket is closed. The
   prompt's clauses tied to W2-21, W2-50, W2-66, W2-67 and W2-76 stand, though all five tickets are closed.
3. **DEC-866 makes each change a marked, swept supersession, so partial changes pile up.** The prompt (line 239) requires a
   marked status line, a sweep and an update for every change. DEC-1157 changes only parts: DEC-1032 "(5) the band", DEC-1018
   (1) "the landing hold", DEC-1039 (1b) "the CPU term". Each leaves the rest of the entry standing. Its "Changes" list leaves
   out DEC-1140 (5) ("the night hold … stays") and (2) (the dispatcher's cap), DEC-1132 (4), DEC-1141 (6) and DEC-946, so
   those still read as in force.
4. **Only the owner may lift them, and the lift-or-remove list goes to the owner first.** DEC-1047 (2): "nothing is lifted
   before the owner decides". DEC-1050: "Nothing else is lifted before the owner decides". DEC-1052's basis: "the list of
   what should be lifted or removed comes to the owner first". DEC-1112 (3) and prompt rule 10 send "changes to the owner's
   standing decisions" to the owner. The prompt is the owner's alone to change (prompt line 7).
5. **The mechanism meant to find these was deferred behind the unrecorded lift.** DEC-1052's weekly basis review with
   measured costs (D-11) is "Not started … After the lift" (DECIDED-NOT-DONE line 294; P1-PLAN lines 26 and 88). The register
   shows no costed holds review between DEC-961's HOLDS-REVIEW-1 (10-30) and DEC-1156 (9)'s inventory (11-12 22:55).
6. **The one list stopped being maintained.** DIF R-9 says "a rule that is not on the list does not exist" (prompt line
   241). But the DIF's header says it was "brought to DEC-1126". Its last commit is `5808126c6` (DEC-1149/1150). DEC-1142 to
   DEC-1148 and DEC-1151 to DEC-1157 are missing, and it still lists DEC-1138's cap and night hold (lines 547-550) and
   DEC-1140's "the night hold stays" (line 560). SNS still says "emergency mode" (line 4) and was last rewritten on 11-11.
7. **The owner's packages were about tickets.** The fifteen decisions of 11-12 before DEC-1157 (DEC-1142 to DEC-1156) are
   almost all ticket-level: P1-A to P1-N, CIT-P byte approvals, and widenings of W2-45, W2-160 and W2-172. The holds came up
   only with DEC-1156 (9) ("the inventory: every mechanism or rule that stops, holds or delays a lead"). Before that, the
