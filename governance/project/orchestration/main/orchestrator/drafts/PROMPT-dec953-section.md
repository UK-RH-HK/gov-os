## 0. DEC-953 (owner, standing, 2026-10-30): build and test the Gov OS first, then dog-food it — this section overrides everything below

The Gov OS's own unfinished features, used piece by piece to govern their own development, became the main cause of stalls and loops (`governance/project/orchestration/packages/AUDIT-GOV-FEATURES-1.md`). Every Gov OS feature is lifted as a gate on this repository's own development until the Gov OS is whole; nothing is deleted from the product, and the lifted features stay built and tested. Where a rule below enforces a lifted feature, it is not in force; `governance/project/orchestration/LIFTED-BY-DEC953.md` lists them, and `DECISIONS-IN-FORCE.md` beside it is the short list of what holds.

- **Closes: the audit's 10 practices, nothing more.** Independent tests first (MR-3); the change's tests on the merged commit in a clean clone, one run at a time under one lock; an independent review for FULL tickets (no staleness rule); the nightly full run; the guard's four rules; gitleaks; git discipline; the disk floor and scratch budgets; a close record; a quick check of changed record files (YAML parse and the placeholder scan). Run them with `.gov-runtime/scratch/orchestrator/tools/close_build.py <W2> [<W2> …]` (one ticket: its own selection; several on one head: one full batch). It writes `governance/project/orchestration/closes/<W2>-<id>.md`, runs `tk close` and commits with `Task: <id>`, `Implements: DEC-953`, `Role: orchestrator`. No `gov close` on this repository's tickets; no close queue, pre-close gate (a)–(h), `record_checks.sh` before record commits, re-pin, ratchet or accepted-history entry, escalation, repair ticket, quiet window, cut-off, CLOSE-PENDING hold, or "no commits during a queue". A red that fails alone refuses the close; one that passes alone is a flaky candidate, named in the record.
- **One test-selection tool at every level** (item 4): `.gov-runtime/scratch/orchestrator/tools/select_tests.py` runs the product's selection (`gov.close.select.plan`) with the governance files (briefs, prompts, the orchestration record and the nightly's folder, the record places, `.gov-runtime/`) left out of the change, and the ticket's own change (a lead's merge base; at close, the ticket's merges). Full only when core code changed. Workers run their own unit tests; leads run their acceptance tests plus the selection; the close runs the same selection on the merged code; the nightly runs everything. Scoped selection stays a product feature, proven with the others.
- **The guard keeps four rules:** the implementer never writes acceptance tests; no reads of the hidden half of an evaluation set or the held-out material; no reads of secrets; no installs without the owner. Everything else is lifted, the command forms included, once the switch-off lands (its proposal goes to the owner, with an ACT where it needs one). Until then, the guard runs as it is.
- **The nightly** is a plain full test run on the integration head (no `gov close`, no report-only gates). A red is investigated at once: the failing tests re-run alone, traced to the change and its cause. A small, clear fix is made on the spot and reported in the morning package; anything needing the owner is a P1 package with a recommendation (a new ticket and its priority, or a fix folded into a current ticket). The red holds no other work. This replaces DEC-938's hold and DEC-951 A.
- **Order of work, subject to the owner's approval of the updated plan:** memory finished and proven (quick wins W2-86, W2-87 and the register-as-one-record defect first, W2-61's measure after each fix; then W2-55, W2-04, W2-05, W2-06, W2-13, W2-15a) and, in parallel, K3 (W2-60a, W2-60b, W2-11, with the roles the three layers need); then the rest of EXAMIN's minimum. Until the owner approves the plan, only DEC-953 items 2 to 5 and memory's quick wins go ahead; other new tickets wait. `gov close` is proved on the proving ground (W2-81) and EXAMIN's copy, not by re-closing this repository's tickets.
- **Three-layer orchestration** (a master orchestrator and a sub-orchestrator per stream) is planned now and starts with the owner's approval, once working memory and the roles (with skills and briefs, loaded at launch) have both landed, in new sessions.
- **Unchanged:** owner-only decisions and P1s as packages with a ping as they arise (DEC-949); the morning package for the night; every ACT pinged with its file; every owner decision recorded and applied under the rule about rules (DEC-851, DEC-866); the status block (DEC-948); the morning handover to a fresh session (DEC-952 item 6); the owner's daily push.
- **Milestones, each with a ping:** (1) the updated WBS and plan; (2) the gates lifted, the guard at four rules, selection proven scoped and the five closes done (W2-71, W2-77, W2-57, W2-03, W2-26), with any ACT; (3) memory landed and proven and the roles, skills and briefs landed.
