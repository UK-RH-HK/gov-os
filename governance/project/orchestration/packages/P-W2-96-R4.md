# P-W2-96-R4: W2-96's last review failed on one finding; a fix needs a third review (P1, DEC-916)

For the owner.

## 1. Context

W2-96 (`DAEO-ouet`, FULL) is the opaque acceptance runner, `gov accept`: the engineer runs the acceptance tests and sees pass or fail, never the tests themselves (DEC-959 item 6). Its KPI 2 says nothing of the tests reaches the caller, "or any file it leaves". Run 2's round has two reviews (DEC-916); a third is the owner's exception.

## 2. What happened

`gov accept DAEO-ouet` passes 106 of 106. Review 3's finding V1 is fixed. Review 4, the round's last, found **W1**: when `gov accept` is stopped (Ctrl-C, a kill) while a suite has started processes in a session of their own, those processes outlive the clean-up, and folders named after the tests stay in the caller's temporary folder. Seven suites here start such processes (W2-96, W2-69, W2-71, W2-50, W2-76, W1-28, W1-32). The reviewer judged it "yes / fails open", following V1's precedent: the folders' names are the tests' names. The lead reproduced it red in 23 of 24 runs.

## 3. Why it matters

The runner exists so the engineer never learns the tests (MR-3, DEC-959 item 6). Names left behind after a stop are a small leak, reached only when a run is stopped mid-suite.

## 4. Options (DEC-105)

- **(a) Apply now:** before its clean-up, `gov` stops and reaps every process the run started, whatever its session (a Linux subreaper); two new cases first (red on the head 23 of 24 here); about 30 to 40 lines; then **a third review of that change alone, the owner's exception (DEC-916)**. About 3 hours.
- **(b) Defer as a Wave 3 row:** one line in the engineer brief ("stop `gov accept` by stopping its whole process tree"). The residual stays: a stop of `gov` alone during those suites leaves empty test-named folders.
- **(c) Re-baseline KPI 2** to cover only processes in the run's own process tree while `gov` runs; the same residual as (b).

## 5. Recommendation

**(a)**, medium-high confidence: the leak is small but it is exactly what the ticket is for, the fix is contained, and EXAMIN's engineers will use the runner. Under (b) or (c) the lead commits review 4's record as a pass and returns DONE at once.

## 6. What it blocks, and what waiting costs

Only W2-96's close. Its resume waits for this decision; nothing else depends on it tonight.
