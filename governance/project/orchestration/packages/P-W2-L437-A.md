## 1. Context

- **DEC-912** revised W1-01's merge rule: a merge whose acceptance files equal git's own merge of its parents passes.
- **Containment's merge reading** (`src/gov/guard/containment_merge.py`, `read_merge`, DEC-410 DP-24) is separate, and DEC-912 did not change it. Under `tests/acceptance/**`, every path that both parents changed against their merge base is the merge commit's "own" change, even when the merge is git's own clean merge.
- **DEC-572's lift**, for paths whose version every bringing commit passes, covers ticket files only, not acceptance tests.
- **The close** (`src/gov/close/command.py`, `_check_containment`) judges every one of the ticket's commits with `judge_commits`. Every finding refuses the close as `CONTAINMENT_FINDING`. No owner decision or accepted-history entry lifts it: `--owner-decision` lifts only the escalation count.

## 2. What happened (read-only, with `w2/integrate`'s code, the pin's code after the re-pin)

- **The live hook (PostToolUse) only flags.** A committed merge's own change is recorded as "flagged, left in place". Its paths are kept out of the restore step, so nothing committed is ever reverted. It restores only uncommitted out-of-scope changes in the working tree. That is what it did at W2-66's merge (`c60c9704`).
- **The close would refuse.** `judge_commits` over every merge commit of each pending ticket (by its Task trailer, as the close selects them) gives:
  - **W2-66:** a finding on `03f470a5` (`tests/acceptance/W1-30/test_w1_30_close.py`);
  - **W2-65:** a finding on `4ef3293a` (`tests/acceptance/W1-26/README.md`, `tests/acceptance/W1-26/test_w1_26_planted_defects.py`);
  - **W2-58, W2-63, W2-70, W2-07, W2-69, W2-67 and W2-59:** no finding, so their closes are not affected.
- **`gov check` stays green** with `03f470a5` in the history: W2-66's run had exit 0 at `d738898f`. The nightly and the re-pin's verification are not affected.

## 3. Why it matters

W2-66 and W2-65 cannot close until containment reads these merges as DEC-912 does. Both are in the first re-pin, so their code is pinned either way; only their closes wait.

## 4. Options (DEC-105)

- **(a) Ticket W2-72 (FULL, engineer, in the guard): containment's merge reading follows DEC-912 for acceptance tests.**
  - The rule: a path both parents changed is not the merge's own change when the merge holds exactly git's own merge of it (merge-tree, no conflict on that file), and every commit that brought each side passes when judged alone. These are DEC-572's lift conditions, widened to `tests/acceptance/`.
  - A hand edit or a conflict resolution stays a finding.
  - **What it could let through:** a clean auto-merge of two passing commits' edits, which nobody typed.
  - **Cost:** about 40 to 60 lines in `src/gov/guard/containment.py`, plus independent tests and review, about 6 to 8 hours.
  - **Timing:** carried by the second re-pin (with W2-68 and W2-71); W2-66 and W2-65 close after it. **Or** in the first re-pin, which moves the re-pin back by about a day.
- **(b) Hold W2-66's and W2-65's closes until W2-45** (the hardening pass) changes the rule. No cost now, but the two closes wait much longer.
- **(c) An exemption list for the close's containment gate,** like `acceptance_exemptions` (exact commits, your Role: owner entry each). A smaller code change, but it needs an owner commit for every such merge, now and later.
