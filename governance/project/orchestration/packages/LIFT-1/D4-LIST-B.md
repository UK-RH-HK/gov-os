## 2. The levers, the opt-in suite and the two tiers

- **Fold** (unit folders; acceptance families whose parameters are the KPI line's own enumerated items, such as W1-07's
  "every command", W1-08's record types, W2-49's Contract items): a parametrised family becomes one case that asserts
  every parameter and names each failing one. The count falls; the assertions and the seconds stay. No KPI line moves.
- **Representatives** (other acceptance families: forms, encodings, offsets, roles of a planted fixture): keep one
  accepted and one refused form per family, plus any form a KPI line names. Count and seconds fall.
- **Cap (tier 2 only):** at most 6 functions per KPI line of the suite's ticket (6 x the ticket's lines). Each line
  keeps the first case its README names (Appendix B), then the red-reason cases; the review-round repeats beyond 6
  per line go. Applied only where a suite holds more than 6 functions per line (33 of my 74 acceptance folders).
- **Delete or merge:** the approved inventory rows (D3, M1, M1b, M3 optional, M5, M7, M9, R1-R5) as decided by
  DEC-1040 (1).
- **Opt-in suite `lifted` (outside the full run; T-10's change):** one list, `tests/optin-lifted.txt`, in the form of
  `nightly_only.txt` (a path or node id, then `#` and why), read by `tests/conftest.py`'s
  `pytest_collection_modifyitems`: its entries are deselected unless the run says `--optin=lifted` (or
  `GOV_TESTS_OPTIN=lifted`). One place, so the nightly, the close's plugin, the leads and `select_tests.py` all leave
  them out without a change to any of them; `nightly_only.txt`'s gitleaks entries move into it (that file then holds
  nothing and can retire; `select_tests.py`'s `leave_out_nightly` stays harmless with an empty list). Appendix A is
  its content. A feature switched back on (W2-115's close, W2-45's families, a scan turned on) takes its lines out of
  the list in that ticket.
