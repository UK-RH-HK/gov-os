# INVESTIGATION-1 / B2: the guard, the hooks, the kernel template, the Contract's ceremonies (DEC-1047)

By a read-only investigator, 2026-11-02 evening. Tree: main, `w2/integrate` `1e02facd`, clean. Nothing was changed in
any tree. Helper scripts and outputs: `/tmp/gov-os-inv-B2/` (`suites.py`, `files.py`, `classify.py`,

## Headline
1. **The guard's five rules are right for the aim; their Bash parsing is not.** Rules 1, 2 and 5 still refuse commands by reading their words. That is the command-form refusal DEC-953 lifted, brought back through the five-rule hook and the product's read judge. The log holds 86 refusals since ACT 1: about 79 false, 4 true, 2 of those 4 caused by a wrong brief. They took four owner operator commits in three days (ACT-1, FIX-1, FIX-2, FIX-3), a 1,431-line parser with 270 tests, and W2-42a, which adds 7,891 lines so far to put the same parser into the product.
2. **Cheaper walls already exist or cost a few lines.** The launcher already gives every sandboxed non-designer worker an `Edit` deny on `tests/acceptance` (the OS sandbox enforces it without parsing). A merge-time check of the `Role:` trailers on commits that touch `tests/acceptance/` would take about 10 lines in `land.py`. Rule 2's hidden half covers every engineer and research session, not only the measured tickets: a one-line path-map change, which W2-61 promised and never made, removes the false refusals.
3. **The product (kernel template) still ships the full guard and post-command containment by default.** Containment snapshots and restores on every Bash call, so it can lose work. By an estimate from file names, about 60 % of the guard family's nightly case time (about 5,300 of 8,800 s) and about half of `src/gov/guard` (about 3,100 of 5,822 lines) serve rules already lifted here. Recommendation: the five rules become the product's default; the rest stays built but off by default; nothing new is built on them (retire or defer W2-42, W2-09, W2-10, W2-44; re-scope W2-42a).
4. **The checkpoint ceremony has two layers that bring nothing back.** The Stop hook writes an automatic checkpoint at every turn end (741 in the main tree, 656 of them the CTO's). That record shadows the lead's own `gov checkpoint` record in the resume brief. The `gov checkpoint` records themselves (345 files, 435 commits, DEC-511) are read only by the lifted close gate. The prose RESUME HERE checkpoint does the real work. Keep it; lift the other two.
5. **The Contract and the Charter still state as mandatory what DEC-953 lifted:** default-deny write scope, containment, stop triggers, trailer traceability, three-iteration loops, a decision record for every answer, and the ratchet lists. The register now holds standing exceptions instead. One Contract re-baseline (DEC-105) after the owner's decision on this list is cheaper than more exceptions.
