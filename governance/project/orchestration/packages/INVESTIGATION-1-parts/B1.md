# INVESTIGATION-1, part B1: the Gov OS product's gates, checks, close and records (DEC-1047)

**By:** a read-only investigator (part B1), 2026-11-02 evening. **Tree read:** `w2/integrate` at `1e02facd` (clean). Nothing was changed. The helper scripts are in `/tmp/gov-os-inv-B1/` (`queue_stats.py`, `costs.py`). Every figure comes from a log, a record or a command named in the evidence section. Estimates are marked **(est.)**.

## Headline
1. **The product's strict `gov close` almost never passed here.** 30 direct runs (7 to 19 Oct) passed 0 times. 15 of them are timed, at 1,098 min together. 32 attempts through the close queue (20 to 28 Oct) passed 18 times and cost 2,060 min of machine time. That is about **53 h of machine time for 18 closes**. No product close has run since 28 Oct (DEC-953). It is still the product's default, it has no switch for any gate, and it is 4,767 lines with about 2,764 acceptance cases (13,382 s) and 1,078 unit cases spent on it and its neighbours.
2. **What DEC-953 lifted here should also leave the product's defaults**, with three exceptions: TREE_NOT_COMMITTED, class and evidence, and the collection check stay on, and the disk floor stays with a smaller default. W2-115's plan (`close: strict` off by default) is the right shape. It should go further: take `src/gov/close/**` and `src/gov/check/**` out of the test map's `shared_core`, and run the strict close's own suites only in the nightly.
3. **`gov check` is all-or-nothing.** It always runs 20 declared checks, 3 built-in checks and the policy-coverage check, one after another. It took 5.7 min on average with an idle machine (n=32) and 15 to 30 min under load (897 to 1,800 s). A check that times out under load turns its family RED and blocks. Scope it to the change and send the four memory-heavy families to the nightly. Lift five ceremony checks. The policy-coverage check turns RED whenever a check is lifted, so it must be lifted first.
4. **The biggest hidden cost is in the test map.** Every scoped selection always adds `record_reading` and `every_close`: 689 cases and 4,675 s of case time. That includes W2-54's "green here" file, which starts a whole `gov check` from inside a test. It cost 897 to 1,800 s in **every one of the 11 close builds measured**. Move both lists to the nightly.
5. **The product's CI and lefthook template.** The CI workflow is red on **42 of 42** pushes since 9 Oct, so nobody can read a signal from it. The lefthook template runs the full G1+G2 `gov check` and two gitleaks scans on every commit of an adopting repository. Lift both from the defaults. The close records are 179 to 636 KB each and are embedded into memory together with 345 checkpoints: slim them and stop embedding them.
