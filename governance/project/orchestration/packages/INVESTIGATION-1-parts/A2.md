**Headline (5 lines)**
1. **Attribution is the costliest item (practice 7).** Of 162 traced reds, 160 gave the same verdict on round 1 as on round 3. 2. **The guard harvest runs every 10 minutes, not every minute (practice 6).** The 1-minute figure is the dispatcher's pass. Each harvest re-reads 2.7 GB (4,188 transcripts) and re-judges every old refusal in a subprocess. It ran 134 times in 23.4 h (mean 30 s, max 117 s) and found 30 new refusals. It earned its keep while the guard was being fixed (79 of 86 refusals were false positives, 29 of 31 fixed). Run it nightly or on demand from now on.
3. **About 1,300 test-started code-graph daemons a day (practice 8).** That is 21,004 per-root daemon folders since 10-19, never cleaned (230 MB). Most come from tests that run `gov rebuild` or `gov adopt` with the real tool on PATH, not from the code-graph tests themselves.
4. **Review effort tracks a hand-set label, not risk (practice 9).** 60 of 136 W2 tickets are FULL, many of them gate or ceremony tickets. On FULL tickets, test design (xhigh) plus review (max) cost $885 against $180 for the engineers (4.9×); on STANDARD the ratio is 1.8×. The xhigh "measured trial" (DEC-839) has no recorded result.
