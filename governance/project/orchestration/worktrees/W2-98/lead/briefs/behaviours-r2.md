**The placeholder class (used by C1 and C3).** A text is a placeholder when, after removing surrounding spaces, Markdown emphasis (`*`, `_`), code-span backticks, surrounding brackets or parentheses and trailing punctuation (`.`, `,`, `;`, `:`, `!`), it is one of these kinds, matched case-insensitively against the whole text (never a word inside a sentence):
- empty, or no letter or digit at all: `-`, `—`, `–`, `?`, `...`, `…`;
- a lone angle-bracket slot: `<owner>`, `<behaviour>`, `<licence>`;
- a deferral: `TBD`, `TBC`, `TBA`, `TODO`, `pending`, `unknown`, or a phrase `to be <word>` (`to be written`, `to be decided`, `to be confirmed`);
- an absence word alone: `none`, `nobody`, `no one`, `n/a`, `na`, `nil`, `null`.
So `TBD`, `Tbd.`, `**TBD**`, `` `TBD` ``, `(none)`, `N/A.`, `NA`, `TBC`, `to be decided`, `nobody`, `...`, `<owner>` are all placeholders; `None of the members sees the page.`, `no page is shown`, `NASA data office`, `Nadia` are not.
