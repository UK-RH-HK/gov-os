- a lone angle-bracket slot: `<owner>`, `<behaviour>`, `<licence>`;
- a deferral: `TBD`, `TBC`, `TBA`, `TODO`, `pending`, `unknown`, or a phrase `to be <word>` (`to be written`, `to be decided`, `to be confirmed`);
