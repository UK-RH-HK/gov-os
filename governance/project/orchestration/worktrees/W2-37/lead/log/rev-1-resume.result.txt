### F1 — MAJOR — Uncommitted or older-branch record state reads as `ok` and `complete: true` (a gate fails open)

**What I did (P1, now confirmed):** in a fixture with a current store and a current code index:
- I added an uncommitted ticket `PROJ-new1` that implements DEC-001;
- I removed DEC-002's `depends_on: DEC-001` without committing;
- I rebuilt the code index, then ran `gov impact DEC-001`.

**What happened:**
- `graphs: {record: ok, code: ok}`, `complete: true`, `gaps: []`, radius R3;
- PROJ-new1 is neither listed nor a gap;
- DEC-002 is still listed as affected;
- nothing mentions uncommitted files;
- `gov closure` on the same repo reports `uncommitted: ['.tickets/PROJ-new1.md', 'docs/adr/DEC-002.md']`.

The first pass also showed the branch-switch case: a store loaded on a newer branch reads `ok` on an older branch. It listed a ticket that is not in the tree and dropped one that is, because "current" only checks that HEAD is among the loaded commits.

**What should happen:** uncommitted changes under record paths, or a store loaded from a commit other than HEAD, give a record graph that is not `ok` (stale) and `complete: false`.

- Ordinary work: yes, because changing a decision and asking its impact before committing is the KPI-1 flow itself, and switching branches after a load is routine.
- Consequence: fails open

### F6 — MAJOR (new) — A live symbol that shares its bare name with a dead one is read as deletable, radius R0 (fails open)

**What I did (P2):**

**What happened:** `gov impact w37_dup` reported:

### F5 — MINOR — `gov impact` is declared "read" but `--out` writes anywhere, unseen by the guard

**What happened:** `--out` wrote:

- Ordinary work: no for the link, `..` and `.git/` (deliberate constructions); yes for a plain out-of-scope path.
