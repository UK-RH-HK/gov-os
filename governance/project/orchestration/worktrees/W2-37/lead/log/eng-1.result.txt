Interface as built:
```
gov impact [--json] [--root <path>] [--out <path>] <change>...
gov impact [--json] [--root <path>] --compare <CIT-E path>
```
- **`<change>`:** a record id, a symbol name, or one question matching "What's / What’s / what is the impact of X[?]".
- **Assessment `result` keys:**
  - `change`: a list of `{id, graph, path, deletable, governance}`
  - `radius` (`R0`–`R3`) and `depth`
  - `depth_source`
  - `affected`: five lists of `{id, graph}`
  - `graphs`: `{record, code}`, each `ok` or `stale:`/`missing:`/`unavailable:` followed by a reason
  - `complete`
  - `gaps`: a list of `{id, reason}`; reasons are `DEPTH_LIMIT_REACHED`, `UNRESOLVED`, `CODE_GRAPH_MISSING`/`_STALE`/`_UNAVAILABLE`, `NO_KIND`
  - `proposal`: CIT-P frontmatter `{id, type, status: PROPOSED, state_class: DERIVED, title, change, radius, depth, affected}`
  - `options`: DEC-105's three `{option, cost}`, only for a closed specification
  - `findings`: entries with `code: CLOSED_SPEC_EDITED_IN_PLACE`
- **`--compare` `result`:** `{proposal, execution, matches, differences: [{id, kind, only_in}]}`.
- **`--out`:** the CIT-P id is the file's stem when that is a valid document id, otherwise `CITP-<sha8>`.
- **Exit codes:** 0 on success; 1 on refusal, with these error codes: `IMPACT_CHANGE_MALFORMED`, `IMPACT_NO_CHANGE`, `IMPACT_CHANGE_UNKNOWN`, `IMPACT_RECORD_GRAPH_MISSING`, `IMPACT_RECORD_GRAPH_UNREADABLE`, `IMPACT_OUT_EXISTS`, `IMPACT_USAGE`, `IMPACT_EXECUTION_MISSING`, `IMPACT_NOT_AN_EXECUTION_RECORD`, `IMPACT_NO_PROPOSAL`, `IMPACT_PROPOSAL_UNKNOWN`, `IMPACT_NOTHING_LISTED`.
